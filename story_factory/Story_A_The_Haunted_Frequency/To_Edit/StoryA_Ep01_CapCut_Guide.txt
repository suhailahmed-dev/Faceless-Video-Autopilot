   ➤ SUBTITLE: Uski behan Sophia ne puranay darakht ke neeche torch ki roshni daali.

   ➤ SUBTITLE: Sophia ne apna silver compass dekha, uski sui tezi se ulti simt ghoom rahi thi.

   ➤ SUBTITLE: Unke gird darakhton ke aik ke bajaye do do saaye banne lage.

   ➤ SUBTITLE: 'Humein abhi wapas sheher bhagna hoga,' Sophia ne ghabra kar kaha.

   ➤ SUBTITLE: Suhail ne radio uthaya, to wo baraf ki tarah thandi thi.

   ➤ SUBTITLE: Achanak radio se cheekh aayi: 'Suhail! Sophia! Usne tumhara pata dhoondh liya hai!'
